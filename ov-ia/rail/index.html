OneVerify left rail

This option puts the top nav and the avatar menu into one left rail. Every tab becomes a subpage with its own URL and the full width of the screen, so a page like Groups keeps its list and detail layout.

The screen moves from the tabs proposal still apply. Based on staging, 29 Sep 2026

Proposed navigation

Click any item in the rail. Organization › Groups and Configurations › Integrations are mocked in full; the other pages show their title and what they hold.

What this changes

  • Tabs become subpages. Each one gets the whole content area and its own URL, so no page has to fit under a tab strip.
  • The avatar menu goes away. Organization and Configurations sit under Settings in the rail. Account and Log out move to the foot of the rail, and Help & Support opens the help center in a new tab.
  • The organization switcher moves to the top of the rail, above everything it scopes.
  • Sections work as an accordion. Checker has no subpages and opens directly. Opening any other section closes the one before it, because with all of them open the rail runs to about 850px and would scroll on a laptop screen.
